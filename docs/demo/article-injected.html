How solar panels work

Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic cells, thin layers of silicon that release electrons when light hits them.

Those electrons flow as direct current. An inverter converts it to the alternating current that homes use, and any surplus can be stored in a battery or sent back to the grid.

Panels work best in direct sun, but they still produce power on cloudy days, just at a lower rate. Keeping them clean and unshaded matters more than the exact angle.
